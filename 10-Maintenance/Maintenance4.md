# Object Detection System

## 10.4 Control Unit Firmware Update

To update the firmware of the control unit, follow the steps below:

1. Download the latest released firmware for the control unit onto a USB drive.  
2. Insert the USB drive into the terminal located on the right side of the TP (Teach Pendant).  
3. Use the version update function of the TP to perform the firmware update.  

![Warning Symbol](../_assets/경고기호.png)  
During the update process, do not turn off the power or remove the USB drive, as this may cause the system to malfunction.
